# Vincenzo (2021)

*Director:* Kim Hee-won  
*Genre:* Crime, Drama, Comedy, Thriller  
*Cast:*  
Song Joong-ki as Vincenzo Cassano  
Jeon Yeo-bin as Hong Cha-young  
Ok Taec-yeon as Jang Jun-woo / Jang Han-seo  
Kwak Dong-yeon as Jang Han-seo  
Kim Yeo-jin as Choi Myung-hee


## Plot Summary
Vincenzo follows the story of Vincenzo Cassano, an Italian lawyer and mafia consigliere who is of Korean descent. After a falling out with his mafia family, he returns to Korea where he gets entangled with a quirky group of people while seeking justice for a large sum of gold hidden in a building. Along the way, he teams up with Hong Cha-young, a clever and ambitious lawyer, to take down corrupt business elites, turning the legal world upside down with his unique blend of mafia tactics and legal expertise.

## Themes
- *Justice vs. Revenge:* Vincenzo’s quest for justice intertwines with his desire for revenge against the corrupt.
- *Good vs. Evil:* The series features sharp contrasts between the morally gray protagonists and the ruthless antagonists.
- *Loyalty and Family:* Despite his cold exterior, Vincenzo demonstrates loyalty to those who stand by him, even as he navigates his own complicated family dynamics.
